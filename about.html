About Us

Thoughtful gifting.
Meaningful connections.

At J&Ko Ventures, we believe that a gift is more than just an object — it is a thoughtful expression of appreciation, recognition, celebration and connection.

We are a professional corporate gifting and customised gifting company dedicated to helping businesses create meaningful experiences for their employees, clients, customers, business partners and stakeholders.

From thoughtfully curated gift hampers to premium customised merchandise, employee welcome kits, festive gifts, promotional products and executive gifts, we provide complete gifting solutions designed around your brand and occasion.

Our goal is simple: to make every corporate gift memorable, purposeful and beautifully presented.

Who We Are

Built to turn gifting into stronger relationships

J&Ko Ventures was created with a vision to transform corporate gifting from a routine business activity into an opportunity to build stronger relationships.

We understand that every business has its own identity, culture, values and audience. That is why we do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. We work closely with our clients to understand their requirements, budget, occasion, brand personality and target recipients before creating the right gifting solution.

Whether you are looking for gifts for corporate events, employee appreciation, client relationships, festive celebrations, conferences, product launches, onboarding, annual celebrations, weddings, milestone achievements or special occasions, our team helps you create gifts that leave a lasting impression.
