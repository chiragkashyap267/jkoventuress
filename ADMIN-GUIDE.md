# Managing your website

A short guide to the admin panel at **jkoventuress.com/admin**

---

## Signing in

Go to **jkoventuress.com/admin**.

The first time, you will be asked to create a password. Choose something at
least 10 characters long and keep it somewhere safe — nobody can look it up for
you, not even your developer. It is stored scrambled, which is what keeps it
safe if the website is ever broken into.

If you forget it, your developer can reset the panel so you can set a new one.

---

## What you can change

### Contact details

Your emails, phone numbers, office address and the founder name.

Change them once here and they update in the footer of **every** page and on
the contact page together. You never have to hunt for them.

One thing to know: **changing the main email also changes where enquiries from
the website form are sent.** If you want enquiries to go somewhere else, this
is the place.

### Page text

The headings and opening paragraphs on each page. Pick a page from the row of
buttons at the top, make your changes, and press Save.

Underneath every box it says *Original:* followed by the wording the site
launched with. **If you ever want to go back, just empty the box and save** —
the original comes straight back.

You may see two pieces of code in some boxes:

- `<br />` starts a new line
- `<em class="metal">word</em>` makes a word gold

Leave them where they are if you like how the page looks. Anything else you
type is shown exactly as written, so you cannot accidentally break a page.

### Images

Replace any photograph on the site. Click **Choose File**, pick a picture, and
press **Replace**.

You do not need to crop or resize anything first — whatever you upload is
adjusted automatically to the right shape, so it will always sit properly in
the page. JPG, PNG and WebP files work, up to 12 MB.

If you change your mind, **Restore original** brings the old photo back.

---

## Undoing a mistake

Every time you save, the panel keeps a copy of how things were just before.

On the **Overview** page you will find a list of these, newest first. Press
**Restore** next to any of them to put the whole site back to how it read at
that moment. The last 30 saves are kept.

---

## What you cannot change here

Colours, fonts, the logo, the page layout and the menu are not editable in the
panel. That is on purpose — they are your brand, and a single wrong character
in a design setting can break how every page looks. Ask your developer for
those; they are quick changes.

---

## Good to know

- Changes appear on the website **immediately** after you press Save. There is
  no separate publish step.
- If you do not see your change, hold **Ctrl** and press **F5** to force your
  browser to fetch the newest version.
- Always use **Sign out** when you are finished, especially on a shared or
  public computer.
